# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in `spectral-eclipse-frontier` a harassment-free, rigorous, and intellectually honest experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, affiliation, or level of experience.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive scientific environment include:
- Grounding critiques of theorems, proofs, code, or numerical claims in reproducible evidence and explicit citations.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive mathematical, software, and methodological feedback.
- Distinguishing clearly between machine-checked facts, exact computations, numerical estimates, and open conjectures.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, personal attacks, or derogatory comments.
- Fabricating data, citations, proofs, or verification outputs.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to Alexander S. Donahue at `0_0@0thernes.art`. All reports will be reviewed fairly and promptly.
